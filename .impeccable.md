## Design Context

### Users

The dashboard is used by PlovPit's internal Strapi administrators and content
operators for a Khmer Christian app. It should help them understand which books
and formats receive attention, identify content with weak follow-through, and
spot catalog-quality work that needs attention.

### Brand Personality

Professional, modern, simple.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a dark, restrained product-dashboard aesthetic. The interface should feel
calm and credible rather than theatrical: clear hierarchy, compact comparisons,
precise labels, and selective color used to direct attention. It should feel
appropriate for focused administrative work and respectful of the app's Khmer
Christian context.

### Design Principles

1. Put decisions before decoration: every chart should reveal a comparison or
   actionable exception.
2. Keep the dark palette calm and readable, using accents sparingly.
3. Prefer direct labels and exact values over visual ambiguity.
4. Use varied density and spacing to separate overview, diagnosis, and action.
5. Preserve accessibility, keyboard operation, reduced motion, and responsive
   access to every important metric.
